Collage Maker

Grid collages, any rows × columns. Everything runs in your browser — no upload, no server, no account.

1. Pick photos

or drag & drop them here — you can add more at any time

    2. Layout

    Cells

    auto cell size = the most common photo size in your selection. contain keeps every photo whole (margins appear where a photo's shape differs from the cell); cover fills the cell and crops the edges. With auto + contain, photos matching the common size land at native resolution — no scaling, no cropping.

    Output

    Collages larger than max size are scaled down proportionally so browsers don't refuse to render them. Raise it for maximum detail.

    3. Download

      Your photos never leave this device: no network request is made with them. Works offline once loaded.

      HEIC/HEIF files can't be decoded by browsers — convert them to JPEG first.
